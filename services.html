First Time at Methodist Church Subukia?

We are delighted to welcome you! Here is what you can expect when you join our congregation for Sunday worship or midweek fellowship.

Warm & Inviting Hospitality

Our greeters will welcome you at the door, help you find a seat, and answer any questions you may have.

Inspiring Sermons

Scripture-centered preaching focused on practical Christian living, faith, grace, and hope in Christ.

Children & Youth Welcome

Dedicated age-appropriate classes during worship where children learn God's Word in a fun, safe environment.

Uplifting Sacred Music

Rich traditional Wesley hymns combined with contemporary African choral songs of worship.

Tea & Social Hour

Stay after the morning service for tea, snacks, and warm conversation to connect with church members.

Worship & Heritage

Vibrant Liturgy & Inspiring Sacred Music

Methodist heritage is deeply rooted in song, prayer, and sacrament. Our services blend classical Wesleyan order with vibrant local expression, creating a sacred space for genuine encounters with God.

Each service features Old Testament, Epistle, and Gospel readings followed by an expository sermon designed to strengthen faith and apply God's word to daily life.
Celebrated regularly in accordance with Methodist tradition. All who seek Christ's grace are warmly invited to partake in the Lord's Supper at our open table.
Featuring classic hymns by Charles Wesley alongside native language choral anthems performed by our dedicated Subukia Circuit Choir.

Voices of Worship

Our circuit choir leads the congregation in harmonious praise every Sunday morning.

Choral Song Festival

Annual circuit hymn competition celebrating our rich musical tradition.
